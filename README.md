# Three Birds Sing Bach

Author: Deming Xu

Design: Three birds sing the three voices of Bach's C minor fugue (BWV 847). You have to pick out, by ear, the moments when a voice sings the do-si-do motif or runs up or down a scale, and answer each one with a key press on the note that follows. The picture only shows what has already been heard, never when to press.

Screen Shot:

![Screen Shot](screenshot.png)

How To Play:

- Space: play / pause. Enter: watch a demo.
- Click the piano roll at the top to jump anywhere in the fugue.
- Each bird has two keys: Q W E (left, middle, right bird) for do-si-do, A S D for scale runs.
- When a bird sings do-si-do (three quick notes: down a step and back up), press its top key on the note right after. The three circles above its head fill in as the notes go by.
- When a bird runs up or down a scale, press its bottom key on the note that ends the run. The steps under its feet fill in as the run goes by.
- On time: the bird pops and rings a bell. Slightly off: no bell, and the bird gets squashed. Missed: crossed eyes. Pressing when nothing is due gets a "?".

Sounds: everything is synthesized in `PlayMode.cpp` at start-up (a sawtooth-like wavetable for the voices, an octave-only bell for the confirmations), no audio files. The score data in `chart.hpp` is generated by `build.py` from the Humdrum kern encoding of the fugue in the [humdrum-tools/bach-wtc](https://github.com/humdrum-tools/bach-wtc) repository.

Build: `node Maekfile.js` with `nest-libs` as a sibling folder (see NEST.md), then `./dist/game`.

This game was built with [NEST](NEST.md).
